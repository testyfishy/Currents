Currents Saver

Saves the article you are reading to your own Currents server.

The same address you open Currents at. It must be reachable from this device — on a tailnet, that means this device is signed in to it.

What this extension can and cannot see It never renders a login form and never reads one, so your university password does not exist in its world. When you click the toolbar button it reuses the session your browser already has, by fetching with credentials: "include" — the same thing that happens when you click the publisher's own download link.

It does nothing until you click. There is no content script watching pages in the background, and the only place it ever sends a file is the address above.
